OUR HERITAGE

Dedication to Perfection in Natural Stone

Sharanya Marbles was established with a singular vision: to deliver luxury stone finishing and installation services that match the architectural splendor of world-class spaces.

Natural stone is a timeless marvel of nature. Whether it is rare Italian Statuario marble, rich granite countertops, or contemporary large-format tiles, every stone surface demands deep technical knowledge, precise leveling, and artistic finesse.

Over the past decade, our skilled artisans and engineers have completed over 500 prestigious residential villas, luxury apartments, and commercial headquarters, earning a reputation for unyielding quality, dependable timelines, and client satisfaction.

WHY SHARANYA MARBLES

The 6 Pillars of Our Craftsmanship

Our reputation is built on six foundational principles that govern every residential and commercial project we undertake.

1. Quality Materials

We focus strictly on high-grade stones, Italian diamond abrasives, premium epoxy grouts, and long-lasting protective crystallizers.

2. Skilled Workmanship

Careful installation, master handling of delicate stone veining, and professional finishing backed by continuous team training.

3. Precision

Accurate laser measurements, laser levelers, minimal joint spacing, and detailed execution for a symmetrical, pristine look.

4. Reliable Service

Clear upfront communication, punctual team arrivals, dependable schedules, and post-service maintenance support.

5. Professional Finish

Clean, high-gloss polished surfaces, mirror reflections, flush joint fillings, and refined chamfered edges that wow on first glance.

6. Customer Satisfaction

We focus on delivering work that customers can be proud of for decades, with 100% satisfaction guaranteed before final handover.

MODERN TECHNOLOGY

Advanced Machinery & Dust-Free Polishing

We invest in the latest European stone processing equipment to ensure clean, efficient, and damage-free execution in occupied and new spaces.

Traditional polishing often creates heavy dust and uneven stone surfaces. At Sharanya Marbles, our multi-stage planetary floor grinders feature integrated water containment and dust extractors, providing a cleaner working environment and a noticeably flatter, glossier floor.
