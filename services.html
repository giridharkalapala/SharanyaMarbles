2. Tiles Fitting

Professional floor and wall tile installation for luxury residences, commercial towers, and modern villas.

Our tile fitting team utilizes laser alignment levels, heavy-duty tile cutters, and zero-lippage leveling clips to ensure razor-straight joints, hollow-free bedding, and long-term crack resistance.

Included Scope:

Floor Tiles
Wall Tiles

SERVICE 03

3. Marble Polishing

Professional marble restoration, diamond grinding, and mirror-shine crystallization.

Marble naturally loses its gloss over time due to foot traffic, micro-abrasions, and acid spills. Our 7-stage diamond abrasive process grinds away imperfections, eliminates lippage, and seals the natural pores to produce a crystalline, glass-like reflection.

Included Scope:

Surface Deep Cleaning
Multi-Grit Grinding
Diamond Pad Polishing
Stain & Etch Removal
Italian Crystallization
Shine & Luster Restoration
Request Marble Polishing Quote
SERVICE 04

4. Granite Fitting

Strong, durable, and millimeter-precise granite installation for kitchens, staircases, and heavy-duty spaces.

Granite requires specialized handling due to its hardness and weight. We execute seamless waterfall miters, precise under-mount sink cutouts, bullnose/chamfer edge profiles, and heavy-load floor bedding.

Included Scope:

Kitchen Countertops
Staircases & Steps
Heavy-Traffic Flooring
Wall Claddings
Commercial Lobbies
Edge Profiling & Polishing
Request Granite Fitting Quote
